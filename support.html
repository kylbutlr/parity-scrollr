Support and troubleshooting

Some pages cannot be shown inside a comparison. Parity Scrollr does not bypass sign-in, bot protection, or site security controls.

A page stays blank or shows a framing warning

End the comparison and restart it with Compatibility mode enabled. If it still fails, open the page directly and confirm that you can access it normally. Pages with their own frame-blocking code, bot challenges, sign-in boundaries, browser-owned pages, and some site policies remain unsupported.

A signed-in page appears signed out

Framed pages can receive different cookie treatment, especially for third-party cookies. Open both sites directly, sign in if appropriate, and try again. Parity Scrollr does not capture, copy, or bypass credentials.

The responsive rendering differs from a device

Viewport controls change CSS width and height only. They do not emulate user agent, device pixel ratio, touch input, orientation APIs, safe-area insets, or hardware. Use real-device or browser device emulation for those checks.

Capture is disabled

Click the Parity Scrollr toolbar icon while the comparison tab is active. This grants temporary capture access for that tab. You can instead enable persistent all-site access in Settings.

Full-page capture fails or looks inconsistent

Keep the comparison tab active until capture finishes. Sticky and fixed elements can repeat because the extension captures multiple scroll positions. Lazy content, animation, video, infinite scroll, or a growing page can prevent a stable result. The capture stops after 60 screenshots and scales extremely tall output to browser-safe image dimensions.

Shopify theme previews

Select the Shopify site profile when comparing theme previews. It temporarily hides recognized Shopify preview bars during full-page capture and restores them afterward. The profile does not alter storefront content or apply to other sites.

Get help

Report an issue on GitHub. Include your browser and extension versions, what you tried, the visible error, whether Compatibility mode was on, and sanitized URLs with private tokens removed. Do not attach confidential captures.
