Parity Scrollr

Privacy

Parity Scrollr does not collect data for the developer. It processes comparison data in your browser and has no accounts, analytics, advertising, telemetry, hosted screenshot storage, or extension-operated servers.

What is handled

  • The two URLs you choose are held in browser session storage while a comparison is being launched or is active.
  • Scroll positions, page dimensions, navigation changes, and matching scrollable elements are processed in memory to coordinate the comparison.
  • Viewport and extension settings are saved in browser local storage.
  • The complete implementation URL is saved only if you enable Remember the complete implementation URL.
  • Capture images are assembled in browser memory and downloaded directly to your device.

Where it is stored

Active URLs and comparison records use temporary browser session storage. Preferences, viewport choices, first-use guidance choices, and an optional remembered implementation URL use local extension storage on this device. Captures are saved to your normal browser download location.

What leaves your device

Parity Scrollr does not send comparison data or captures to the developer or to an extension server. The pages you compare still communicate with their own servers and third parties according to their own privacy policies. If you turn on Query + hash, those URL details can be copied from one selected site to the other. A downloaded capture leaves your device only if you choose to share it.

How to delete it

End active comparisons, then choose Settings, Clear all local data. This removes saved settings, URLs, first-use choices, temporary comparison records, and optional site-access grants. It does not delete PNG files already downloaded to your device. You can delete those files from your browser download folder.

Site access and Compatibility mode

By default, Parity Scrollr asks for access only to the two selected sites and releases unused grants after the comparison ends. Persistent all-site access is optional in Settings. Standard mode leaves site protections unchanged. Compatibility mode temporarily relaxes framing protection only for the selected sites inside that comparison tab. The temporary rules are removed when the comparison ends, the tab closes, or it navigates away.
